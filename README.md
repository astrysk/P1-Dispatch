# Dispatch

Dispatch is an exploration of pickup reliability in India's manufacturing logistics ecosystem. Researching how small-scale manufacturers can reduce missed pickups, minimize coordination overhead, and ensure dependable outbound deliveries through better logistics coordination, accountability, and exception recovery.

## What This Covers

The work follows the complete product development lifecycle, from initial research to building, testing, and improving the solution.

1. **Problem Discovery & Research:** Understand the problem, target users, market, user needs, and existing solutions.
2. **Product Strategy & Definition:** Define the product vision, goals, value proposition, requirements, MVP scope, and roadmap.
3. **Product Design & User Experience:** Map user journeys, workflows, wireframes, prototypes, and interface designs.
4. **Technical Research & System Architecture:** Define technical feasibility, technology stack, system architecture, data models, APIs, and integrations.
5. **Engineering & Implementation:** Build the product, implement core features, manage code, and document technical decisions.
6. **Testing, Validation & Quality Assurance:** Test functionality, identify issues, evaluate performance, and verify expected behavior.
7. **Business Model & Commercial Strategy:** Define the business model, target customers, market positioning, pricing, and unit economics.
8. **Go-to-Market, Marketing & Distribution:** Define customer acquisition, product messaging, marketing assets, sales strategy, distribution channels, and launch plan.
9. **Deployment & Release:** Prepare the production environment, configure deployment, establish monitoring, and manage product releases.
10. **Open Source & Developer Documentation:** Prepare the README, installation guide, API documentation, licensing, troubleshooting, and contribution guidelines.
11. **Results, Evaluation & Iteration:** Evaluate product performance, analyze user feedback, measure outcomes, and plan future improvements.
12. **Project Case Study & Publication:** Document the project journey, demonstrate the product, publish project details, and record outcomes and learnings.
