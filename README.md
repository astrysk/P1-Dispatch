# Dispatch

Dispatch is an exploration of pickup reliability in India's manufacturing logistics ecosystem. Researching how small-scale manufacturers can reduce missed pickups, minimize coordination overhead, and ensure dependable outbound deliveries through better logistics coordination, accountability, and exception recovery.

## What This Covers

The work follows the complete product development lifecycle, from initial research to building, testing, and improving the solution.

1. **Problem Discovery & Research:** Understand the problem, target users, market, and existing solutions.
2. **Product Strategy:** Define the product vision, goals, target customers, and value proposition.
3. **Requirements & Planning:** Identify features, user stories, priorities, and product scope.
4. **UI/UX Design:** Map user journeys, workflows, wireframes, and interface designs.
5. **Technical Architecture:** Define the system architecture, technology stack, and data structure.
6. **Development:** Build the product and its core features.
7. **Testing & Quality Assurance:** Test functionality, identify issues, and verify expected behavior.
8. **Business Model & Go-to-Market:** Explore pricing, revenue streams, customer acquisition, and launch strategy.
9. **Deployment & Launch:** Prepare the solution for release.
10. **Iteration & Improvement:** Use feedback and results to improve the product.
11. **Documentation & Case Study:** Record decisions, development progress, outcomes, and lessons learned.
